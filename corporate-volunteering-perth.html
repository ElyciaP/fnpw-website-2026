Volunteering days in Perth

Western Australia   ·   9:30am to 2:30pm

One site, forty acres of bushland, and three species of black cockatoo that depend on it.

Where you would work

One site, and it needs the help.

Wildlife care

Kaarakin Black Cockatoo Conservation Centre

Kaarakin sits on 40 acres of bushland in the Perth Hills and is home to Carnaby’s, Baudin’s and Forest Red-tailed black cockatoos, all under pressure from habitat loss and urban development.

Your team prepares and distributes food for the cockatoos, cleans aviaries and care areas, and helps with site and bushland maintenance. There is no planting here at all, this one is wildlife care.
