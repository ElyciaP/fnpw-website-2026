Partnering for impact

Together, we can achieve more for Australia’s wild places.

Protecting land, recovering species and restoring landscapes at scale is more than any one organisation can do alone. It takes Traditional Custodians, governments, philanthropists, businesses and delivery partners all working towards the same outcome.

Together we’ve already passed our goal of adding 10,000 hectares to protected areas. By 2028 we aim to secure $50 million in funding from corporates, government and philanthropy and build at least 20 high-impact partnerships, and we’d like you to be one of them.

Ways to partner

Find the partnership that fits.

01

Fund Conservation at Scale

Fund the acquisition of land for national parks, the growing and planting of native habitat, or recovery work for a threatened species. Every project starts with an agreed scope and the outcomes your funding will deliver, whether that’s hectares protected, habitat restored or animals treated.

02

Build Conservation Partnerships

The most lasting results take years, not a single funding round. We build long-term partnerships around a landscape, a species or a cause that matters to you, shaping the program together and growing it as the results come in.

03

Invest in Nature’s Future

We’re exploring how public, philanthropic and private capital could work together on long-term landscape outcomes, including through nature finance. This work is still being developed, and we’d welcome a conversation with organisations who want to help shape it.

How we work

We do more than fund the work. We make it happen.

  1. 01

    Identify

    With Traditional Custodians, scientists and government, we find the places and species where a partnership will make the biggest difference, and shape them into projects ready for funding.

  2. 02

    Deliver

    We bring the partners and funding together, then engage specialists on the ground, from Traditional Owner ranger groups to community nurseries and wildlife organisations, against clear milestones.

  3. 03

    Demonstrate

    We track the ecological and financial outcomes and report back, so you can see what your partnership achieved and where it could go next.

Opportunities

Projects ready for funding now.

These are some of the projects where partner funding can make the biggest difference right now. Each one is already under way, with results to show.

Growing National Parks · SA

Remarkable Southern Flinders

Four reserves in the Southern Flinders Ranges have been joined into one park of around 30,000 hectares, co-managed with the Nukunu people. Partner support now helps people experience this landscape while protecting its ecological and cultural values.

Funding supports
Trails, campgrounds and visitor facilities designed to protect the landscape
So far
More than 430 hectares added to the park in 2024
Read about the project →
Saving Species · SA

Bandicoot Superhighway

The Southern Brown Bandicoot is the last of the eight bandicoot species South Australia once had, and clearing has stranded it in isolated pockets of habitat. This project plants corridors across the gaps in the Mount Lofty Ranges and Fleurieu Peninsula so it can move between them again.

Funding supports
Corridor planting, habitat management and planned translocation trials
So far
34,669 seedlings planted across 44 hectares
Read about the project →
Healing the Land · National

Landscape Resilience Program

What began as a promise to plant a million trees after the 2019–20 bushfires passed that mark in 2024. The goal now is 9 million trees by 2030, grown and planted with community nurseries to restore habitat and make landscapes more resilient.

Funding supports
Seed collection, community nurseries and planting
So far
1.2 million trees, shrubs and seedlings planted
Read about the project →
Healing the Land · VIC, SA and NSW

Fire Wise

Fire Wise helps communities prepare for bushfire by planting low-flammability natives instead of clearing habitat. Its first phase is complete, and partner funding can take it to more communities.

Funding supports
Workshops, community nurseries and Fire Wise plantings
So far
4,900+ people trained across three states
Read about the project →

Partnerships in action

What our partnerships have achieved.

Each of these began differently, with a business, a government agency and a gift in a will, and each has changed what is possible on the ground.

Corporate
15 ha
restored on Hindmarsh Island in 2025, with 10,000 local provenance plants
L’OCCITANE Australia · since 2020

Restoring a Ramsar wetland on Hindmarsh Island

L’OCCITANE has partnered with us since 2020, when they helped fund our first Bushfire Recovery Nurseries. In 2025 their $90,000 contribution helped restore coastal woodland and wetland across five sites on Hindmarsh Island (Kumarangk), alongside the Ngarrindjeri, the Kumarangk Hindmarsh Island Landcare Group, local nurseries and park managers.

Read the full story →
Government
18,582
wombat mange treatments, nearly double the 10,000 target
NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service

Curb Wombat Mange Program

Together we put up to $2 million into rapid response grants between 2023 and 2025, getting funding to Indigenous community organisations and licensed wildlife groups across NSW. An estimated 1,498 wombats were treated in the first large-scale targeted program of its kind in the state.

Read the full story →
Philanthropy
1,900 ha
secured in 2025, now being added to Coorong National Park
With the Ngarrindjeri Aboriginal Corporation and the Australian and SA Governments

Mundoo Island Station

When this rare land at the Murray Mouth came up for sale, we joined the Australian and South Australian Governments as an acquisition partner, with a substantial part of our contribution coming from a gift in a will. The islands are now being added to Coorong National Park, protecting habitat for 65 nationally listed threatened species, and their management plan is being developed with the Ngarrindjeri Aboriginal Corporation.
