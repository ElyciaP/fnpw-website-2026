Partner & invest

Turn your conservation goals into protected landscapes.

We bring First Nations organisations, government, communities, conservation experts and private funding together to protect Australia’s most important landscapes and wildlife. How we work

We find the opportunity, build the partnership and see it through to measurable impact.

  1. 01

    Identify

    We work with First Nations groups, scientists and local stakeholders to identify high-value ecosystems and the species that depend on them.

  2. 02

    Structure

    We build the partnership and the funding around each opportunity, bringing government, philanthropy and business together.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    We support delivery on the ground, with First Nations knowledge and modern science working side by side.

  4. 04

    Demonstrate

    We measure the outcomes and report them every year in our Impact Report.

First Nations organisations

Lead the work on Country, with us walking alongside you.

We recognise and honour First Nations peoples as the original and ongoing custodians of Country. We co-design projects that protect ecosystems and respect cultural heritage, from co-managed parks to traditional land care practices, and we commit to long-term, respectful collaboration rather than one-off projects.

Our commitment. Our Reconciliation Action Plan sets out how we work with First Nations peoples and communities. Find out more

What we bring

  • Respectful co-management of parklands and protected areas
  • Projects that bring traditional knowledge and scientific expertise together
  • Community-based nurseries, fire-smart planting and ecological restoration

Government

Deliver your biodiversity commitments with a partner that measures results.

We have partnered with governments for more than five decades to deliver on-ground, measurable conservation outcomes. Our science-led programs protect threatened species, grow national parks and restore climate-resilient landscapes, and they align directly with national frameworks including 30 by 30.

Track record. Proven delivery through projects like Nilpena Ediacara National Park and Fire Wise. Find out more

What we bring

  • Projects aligned with 30 by 30 and with national and state biodiversity strategies
  • First Nations knowledge built in, delivering ecological, social and economic co-benefits
  • Transparent reporting that shows what each project achieved

Foundations, PAFs & trusts

Fund strategic conservation that fits your mission and proves its results.

We partner with foundations to co-design and deliver high-impact conservation across biodiversity, climate resilience and community wellbeing. Our approach blends modern science with traditional knowledge, backed by strong governance and transparent reporting on both environmental and social outcomes.

Due diligence. Our strategy, audited financial statements and impact reports are published for you to review. Find out more

What we bring

  • Projects matched to your foundation’s priorities
  • Multi-year programs you can co-design with us
  • Case studies and before-and-after evidence of impact

Major donors & philanthropists

Build a conservation legacy you can see.

We work closely with major donors to bring their conservation values to life. Each gift is matched with strategic initiatives that reflect your passions and priorities, from tree planting and species recovery to national park expansion, and we keep you close to the results with personalised updates and tailored reporting.

See it in action. A gift in a Will helped us secure Mundoo Island Station for Coorong National Park. Find out more

What we bring

  • Giving matched to the places and species you care about
  • Tailored reporting, including case studies and before-and-after results
  • Gifts in Wills that can fund land acquisition, species recovery or nursery programs

International funders

Back globally significant biodiversity, delivered on the ground in Australia.

Our continent is home to some of the world’s most unique and threatened species. We offer international funders a clear pathway to contribute to global biodiversity and climate goals through trusted, on-ground work that restores landscapes, supports threatened species, expands protected areas and empowers Indigenous-led conservation.

Shared goal. We are committed to helping protect 30% of Australia’s land and sea by 2030. Find out more

What we bring

  • Projects aligned with the UN SDGs and the Global Biodiversity Framework
  • Transparent, data-driven reporting on ecological and community impact
  • More than 55 years of conservation experience in Australia

In good company

Case study · Our model in action

Mundoo Island Station: from farmland to national park.

In 2026, 1,900 hectares at the Murray Mouth became part of Coorong National Park. Here is how our model worked in one place.

Identify
Land at the Murray Mouth does not come up for sale. When Mundoo Island Station did, the window to secure it was narrow.
Structure
We joined the Australian and South Australian Governments as an acquisition partner, with a substantial part of our contribution coming from a gift in a Will.
Deliver
The management plan is being developed with the Ngarrindjeri Aboriginal Corporation and the South Australian Government.
Demonstrate
Up to 65 nationally listed threatened species protected, counting toward 30 by 30.
