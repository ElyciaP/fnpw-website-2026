Fundraise with us

Make a difference for wildlife and conservation.

Whether it’s a bake sale, a fun run or a morning tea at work, every fundraiser helps protect Australia’s wild places and the wildlife that call them home.

Getting started

Your idea, our support.

Tell us what you have in mind and we will help you make it happen. Start with our Fundraising Guidelines, then fill out the Proposal to Fundraise so we know what you are planning.

If you would like some inspiration first, the ideas below and our Community Fundraising Kit are a good place to begin. When you are ready, use the form at the bottom of this page to tell us about your plans.

Ideas to get you started

Pick a fundraiser that suits you.

Some people bake, some people run and some turn a workplace morning tea into a cause. Here are a few ideas to spark your own.

Community event

Hold a garage sale, bake sale or sausage sizzle in your neighbourhood.

Workplace event

Host a morning tea, or ask colleagues to donate the cost of their coffee.

Raffle or auction

Gather donated prizes, then sell raffle tickets or auction them off on the night.

Sale of products

Make something you love and donate the proceeds from every sale.

Challenge event

Run, ride or hike a distance that tests you, and ask people to sponsor you.

School fundraiser

Rally the whole school behind a cause, or run a stall at the next fete.

You will find plenty more ideas in our Community Fundraising Kit.

Two ways in

Start your own event, or join one that’s already running.

Create your own event

You might collect donations in lieu of wedding presents, set yourself a personal challenge or simply ask friends to give. Each of these makes a real difference for Australia’s wilderness and wildlife.

You can set up your own fundraising page and raise money securely online through GoFundraise, or directly with us. Get in touch and we will help you set it up.

Join an existing event

Marathons, ocean swims, bike rides and plenty of other challenge events welcome charity fundraisers. Take one on yourself, or put together a team of colleagues, friends or family so everyone can be part of it.

We can also set up a fundraising page for your company or team, which collects donations securely online and sends your donors their receipts automatically. Get in touch to find out more.

We cannot thank you enough

For loving Australia the way you do.

Thank you for taking that extra step for Australia’s environment. Together we can protect habitat and the wildlife that depends on it, and leave something future generations will be glad we did.

We can’t wait to hear what you have planned.
