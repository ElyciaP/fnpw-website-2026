Where your money goes.

Every donation is put to work across our three pillars. Here is what that looks like.

Where your money goes

Your support has an enormous impact on conservation.

We rely on your help to fund critical conservation projects Australia-wide, partnering with community groups, rangers and scientists, First Nations groups, individuals and government agencies to have a direct impact on growing and protecting natural Australia.

01

Projects to protect our future environment

We support a range of projects to protect natural Australia for future generations. Since the 2019 and 2020 bushfires we have focused on healing our land in bushfire affected areas of Australia.

To see the impact we have on our environment, take a look at the diverse range of projects we are currently involved in.

See our projects →

84 projects currently funded across every state and territory.

02

Land acquisitions to grow our national parks

To conserve nature, we must preserve the land and water of our country, the lifeblood of environmental balance.

Species, including our own, cannot survive without a healthy habitat. That is why we add land to Australia’s national parks for permanent protection. We are not about locking nature up. We are about protecting it in the most secure way possible.

How we grow national parks →

29,479 hectares added to national park status in the last year, including the 1,900 hectares of Mundoo Island secured for Coorong National Park.

03

Grants that make a difference

We provide grants for conservation projects run by government agencies, private landholders, community groups and scientists.

Are you planning a project that will help conserve Australia’s native plants, animals or cultural heritage? A grant from us could help you get it off the ground.

Ask about grants →

Our Community Conservation Grants have funded work from seed collection to species monitoring.

04

Environmental education for future generations

Learning about what you see outside encourages you to care, and to practise more positive environmental behaviours.

We believe environmental education is central to the projects and grants we fund. Backyard Buddies is our best known educational initiative.

See Backyard Buddies →

1,215,443 trees, shrubs and seedlings planted across priority landscapes, many of them grown by community nurseries and school groups.
