Corporate governance.

We are accountable to our supporters, our partners and the ACNC. Here is how the organisation is run.

Company limited by guarantee
  • We were incorporated on 29 June 1970 as a company limited by guarantee ACN 000 744 516, regulated by the Australian Securities and Investment Commission to protect consumers, investors and creditors.
  • We were established as a national not-for-profit organisation to grow appreciation of and raise funds for the benefit of the natural environment and cultural heritage of Australia, under our Constitution as amended 2 February 2000.

How we are governed

  • We have a voluntary, unremunerated President, Treasurer and Secretary, and Board of Directors. These positions are elected annually to oversee our activities.
  • The Board is supported by the Finance & Risk Committee, Fundraising & Marketing Committee, Projects Committee, and Nominations Committee. The committees are comprised of staff, board members, and external representatives as appropriate.
  • The Board meets six times a year and its Committees meet between three and six times a year. They are guided by our Constitution, corporate strategies, annual business plans, as well as supporting marketing and other strategies and plans.

Reporting and accountability

  • We provide an annual report on the previous year’s performance with externally audited financial accounts to our Members, the Governors and Life Members, at our Annual General Meeting.
  • A copy of our annual report and financial statement is provided to the Commonwealth Department of Environment and Heritage as part of our statutory requirements.
  • As a national entity, we also seek approval in each relevant State and Territory to undertake fundraising activities.

Registrations and tax status

  • We are registered with the Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission (ACNC) as a charity focused on advancing the natural environment.
  • We hold an Authority to Fundraise for Charitable Purposes with the Department of Gaming and Racing.
  • We are registered with the Australian Taxation Office for Goods and Services Tax (GST), ABN 51 248 905 949.
  • We are endorsed by the Australian Taxation Office as a Deductible Gift Recipient (DGR) as a public museum, and as an Income Tax Exempt Charitable Entity (ITEC) as a charitable institution.
  • We are exempted by the Office of State Revenue from pay-roll tax.

Related entities

  • We are the Trustee of the FNPW Gift Fund Trust ABN 42 651 974 301 (Deed of Settlement 2 February 2000, DGR, ITEC), entered into the Register of Environmental Organisations kept by the Commonwealth Department of Environment and Heritage on 9 February 2001 and entitled to receive tax deductible donations of money and property.
  • We are the registered office of The Gondwana Program BRN T2383504, a membership loyalty program for national parks and wildlife supporters.
  • We are the registered office of Friends of National Parks BRN R3566341, a community-based volunteer program for conservation of national parks and wildlife.
