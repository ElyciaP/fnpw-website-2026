Rewilding

2022-10-18  ·  2 min read

Through on going assistance from FNPW we have been able to continue the legacy of regenerating our family property now for three generations.
This year we were able to plant Koala and Glossy Red Tailed Black Cockatoo feed tress throughout a large regeneration area on the eastern section of the property. We are grateful for every bit of assistance following the 19/20 Black Summer bushfires that devastated the property.

Thanks again!
