Thank you for your interest in the Foundation for National Parks & Wildlife! With your support, we’re making a difference for Australia’s incredible nature.

Who We Are?

Since 1970, we’ve been a nonprofit working to protect Australia’s unique wildlife, landscapes, and heritage. We partner with scientists, First Nations communities, and local groups to restore habitats, protect species, and create a sustainable future for all.

Why We Do It?

Australia’s nature is at risk from habitat loss, climate change, and natural disasters. Our mission is to protect biodiversity because each species, forest, and wetland plays a role in keeping our planet healthy, and we don’t want to lose that.
