Sydney Harbour National Park
Bushland restoration

Sydney Harbour National Park

Across the Sydney sites your team removes invasive plants, restores bushland and wetland habitat, and grows natives in the community nurseries. Planting is seasonal and only runs at some sites, so the activity follows what your chosen site needs on the day.
