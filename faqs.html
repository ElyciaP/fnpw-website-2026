About us

What does FNPW stand for?

FNPW stands for Foundation for National Parks & Wildlife.

Are you part of the government?

No. We are a not-for-profit company limited by guarantee, regulated by ASIC, registered as a charity with the Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission and endorsed as a Deductible Gift Recipient. We work closely with federal and state governments and national parks agencies across Australia, but we are an independent, apolitical charity that relies on the generosity of our supporters and partners.

What do you do?

Our mission is to create powerful partnerships that protect and restore Australia’s unique natural environment, and our vision is to be the natural partner for the protection of Australia’s priceless biodiversity. Our work sits across three pillars: Growing National Parks, Saving Species and Healing the Land.

Conservation on this scale cannot be done alone, so we bring First Nations communities, scientists, government agencies, corporates and everyday Australians together to care for Country. Our work blends modern science with 65,000 years of Indigenous knowledge, focuses on high-impact regions and species under threat, and supports Australia’s commitment to protect 30 per cent of its land and sea by 2030.

What difference have you made?

Our 2025 Impact Report records 29,479 hectares of land added to National Park status, including the 1,900 hectares of Mundoo Island now part of Coorong National Park. It also records 647 hectares under restoration during 2025, and 1,215,443 trees, shrubs and seedlings planted in key priority landscapes, many of them grown by our network of more than 30 community nurseries.

Who leads the organisation?

Ian Darbyshire is our Chief Executive Officer and Kelly O’Shanassy is our Chair. You can meet our leadership team and Board on our About page.

Where are your offices?

Our head office is in Sydney, at Level 6, 10 Bridge Street, Sydney NSW 2000, and our postal address is GPO Box 2666, Sydney NSW 2001. Our projects and partners reach right across Australia.

What is your ABN and legal name?

Our legal name is Foundation for National Parks & Wildlife and our ABN is 90 107 744 771.

Donating

How can I make a donation?

You can donate online as a one-off gift or a monthly gift, or call us on 1800 898 626. You can also give through workplace giving, or give a tree in someone’s name through Give a Tree.

Is my donation tax deductible?

Yes. As a Deductible Gift Recipient, donations of $2 or more to us are tax deductible in Australia.

How much of my donation goes to conservation?

In 2025 we invested $3.8 million in our core purpose, and our 2025 Impact Report records 80 per cent delivery of purpose. Our financial statements are published every year, so you can see how funds are used.

Can I see your financial statements?

Yes. Our financial statements and annual reports are on our Reports page, and they are also published on the ACNC Charity Register.

Can I give monthly?

Yes. Choose Monthly on our donation page and you will join our Habitat Heroes, the supporters whose regular gifts keep conservation going all year round. Your first gift is processed when you sign up, and later gifts are processed each month around that date.

Can I donate in honour or in memory of someone?

Yes. Give a Tree lets you plant a native tree in someone’s name, and we send them a certificate. For other gifts in honour or in memory of a loved one, please get in touch and we will help.

How can I leave a gift in my Will?

Our bequests page explains the types of gift you can leave and the wording your solicitor will need, and you can download our bequest brochure there. To talk it through in confidence, contact Helen Dusevic on 1800 898 626 or fnpw@fnpw.org.au.

Can I donate land?

Yes, and gifts of land are among the most lasting gifts anyone can make. Our Donate Land page explains how it works and who to speak with.

Receipts and your details

Why haven’t I received a receipt for my donation?

Receipts for online donations are sent by email, so please check your junk or spam folder first. If you give monthly as a Habitat Hero, we send a single annual receipt each July covering the financial year. If you still cannot find your receipt, call us on 1800 898 626 or email fnpw@fnpw.org.au and we will send you a copy.

How do I update my details or change my monthly gift?

Call us on 1800 898 626 or email fnpw@fnpw.org.au and we will update your details, or change or stop your monthly gift. You can read how we look after your information in our privacy policy.

Getting involved

Can I volunteer with you?

We run corporate volunteering days, where teams plant and restore habitat at sites across New South Wales, Victoria, South Australia, Western Australia and Queensland. If you would like to volunteer as an individual, your local national park or community conservation group is a great place to start.

How can I fundraise for you?

Our fundraising page has ideas to get you started and a Proposal to Fundraise form. Send it through and our team will be in touch.

How can my business partner with you?

We work with businesses on partnerships built around real, measurable conservation outcomes. Visit Become a Partner to see how we work together and who to contact.

Do you offer grants?

Yes. We provide grants for conservation projects run by community groups, scientists, private landholders and government agencies. When a grant round opens, we share the details and how to apply in our news and our newsletter.

Do you have a newsletter?

Yes. Our email newsletter brings you project updates, species spotlights and ways to help, straight from the bush. You can sign up here.

Still have a question?

We are happy to help.

Call us on 1800 898 626 or email fnpw@fnpw.org.au, and a member of our team will get back to you. You can also write to us at GPO Box 2666, Sydney NSW 2001.
