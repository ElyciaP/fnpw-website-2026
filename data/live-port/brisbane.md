# Corporate Volunteering in Brisbane, QLD

We acknowledge the Traditional Owners of Country throughout Australia and pay our respects to Elders past and present of the lands and waterways of the area now known as Queensland.

## Corporate Volunteering at Daisy Hill Conservation Park

**Location:** Daisy Hill Rd (approx. 25km from Brisbane CBD)
**Timing:** Weekdays, typically 8:30am – 2:00pm

Bring your team together for a hands-on corporate volunteering opportunity in Brisbane at Daisy Hill Conservation Park, one of Southeast Queensland's most important conservation areas.

Surrounded by eucalypt forest and rich wildlife habitat, Daisy Hill plays a critical role in koala conservation and bushland protection. Corporate volunteering here gives your team the opportunity to contribute directly to wildlife habitat health while building connection, purpose and environmental awareness.

## Why corporate volunteering at Daisy Hill matters?

Daisy Hill Conservation Park is a vital refuge in Southeast Queensland, supporting a large population of native wildlife and providing essential habitat for koalas.

In a boost for conservation, the Queensland Government recently announced a 213-hectare expansion of the park, significantly increasing protected land and opportunities for ecological restoration. This makes now an ideal time for organisations to get involved and support long-term conservation outcomes through volunteering.

The park also serves as an important space for environmental education and community connection. By volunteering at Daisy Hill, your team helps protect biodiversity while contributing to a healthier, more resilient landscape in QLD.

## What your team will do

Corporate volunteering days at Daisy Hill are practical, educational and rewarding. Activities may include:

- Learning to identify native plants and invasive weeds
- Weeding and land care to support habitat health
- Assisting with bushland restoration activities
- Restoring bushland and wetland habitats
- An exclusive visit to the Daisy Hill Koala Centre, learning about koalas, their habitats and current conservation challenges

## FAQs

### What is the cost, and what does it include?

The cost is **$3,500 + GST** for a group of 10–20 people. This amount is **fully tax-deductible** and directly funds our projects across Australia.

If catering is required at **NSW National Parks**, the cost is **$3,700 + GST** (also tax-deductible).

The participation fee covers all necessary arrangements to provide a safe, educational, and impactful volunteering experience:

- **Expert Guidance**: On-site coordination by experienced rangers and conservation staff.
- **Equipment**: All tools, gloves, and safety gear required for the day's activities.
- **Materials**: Native plants, seeds, and other materials essential for restoration projects.
- **Insurance & Permits**: Comprehensive coverage and necessary approvals for all activities.
- **Educational Briefings**: Information sessions on local ecosystems, conservation efforts, and the significance of the work being undertaken.

By participating, your organisation supports FNPW's ongoing conservation initiatives and contributes to the resilience of Australia's nature.

### Why is the volunteering experience paid for?

Corporate Volunteering is paid for due to its substantial coordination, resource allocation and cost recovery. Companies invest in these experiences to foster team cohesion, provide meaningful engagement, and develop employees' connection to nature. Additionally, it supports alignment with CSR/ESG goals, which helps to enhance Corporates' brand reputation and contribute to their sustainability initiatives. This experience is a strategic investment in both employee development and environmental impact.

### Why is the corporate group size small?

Group sizes for corporate volunteering with FNPW are kept small to enhance hands-on participation, foster a deeper connection to the environment and facilitate a better learning opportunity. Smaller groups ensure efficient coordination, meaningful contributions from every participant, and ease in logistical arrangements, ensuring each volunteer has a relevant, educational, and impactful experience in conservation efforts.

### How much notice is required to book a corporate volunteering day?

We require **21 days' prior notice** to arrange a corporate volunteering day.

### Can you provide free volunteer opportunities?

Unfortunately, we are **unable to offer free volunteer work**. As a charity, we rely on donations and corporate volunteering events to help fund vital programs across Australia.

### What are the timings for corporate volunteering days?

Corporate days run on **weekdays only**, approximately 9:00 am – 1:30/2:00 pm (timings vary by state):

- **NSW:** 9:30am – 2:00pm
- **VIC:** 9:30am – 2:00pm
- **QLD:** 8:30am – 2:00pm
- **SA:** 9:30am – 2:30pm
- **WA:** 9:30am – 2:30pm

### Do you offer weekend volunteering?

No, we do not run corporate volunteering events on weekends.

### Can smaller groups or half-day sessions be arranged?

Yes, we can arrange **half-day sessions (3 hours)** and groups of fewer than 10 people. However, the cost of the day remains **$3,500 + GST**. Smaller groups may be combined with other corporate teams.

### Is equipment provided?

Yes, all equipment required for the activities is provided on the day.

### Is catering included?

Yes, catering is included. For NSW National Parks sites, if catering is required, the total cost is **$3,700 + GST**.

### What is the postponement and cancellation policy?

If extreme weather or unsafe conditions arise, FNPW and Rangers will notify you as soon as possible of any postponement.

If you decide to cancel for other reasons, the following fees apply:

- **21+ days:** 0% cancellation fee
- **8–20 days:** 25% cancellation fee
- **4–7 days:** 50% cancellation fee
- **0–3 days:** 100% cancellation fee

### Can my company plant trees?

Tree planting corporate volunteering can only occur at certain times of the year, typically from June to September, and varies by location. Tree planting is less common in sites closer to the CBD. While tree planting may not always be an option, there are other impactful activities available, such as supporting recovery nurseries and participating in restoration sessions in national parks. These activities provide hands-on educational experiences, contribute to environmental resilience and support local ecosystems.

### Why volunteer with FNPW?

Volunteering with the Foundation for National Parks & Wildlife (FNPW) offers a unique opportunity to contribute to conservation and the environment in Australia. Through corporate volunteering days in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Adelaide, teams can engage in hands-on activities to restore habitats, save species and care for our national and urban parks.

### What are the benefits from the day?

#### Volunteering for employees:

- **Connect with Nature** – Experience and appreciate Australia's natural spaces firsthand.
- **Make a Real Impact** – Contribute to environmental restoration and biodiversity conservation.
- **Strengthen Team Bonds** – Engage in hands-on, meaningful team-building activities.
- **Learn & Grow** – Gain insights into local ecosystems, native plants, and wildlife.

#### Volunteering for businesses:

#### Volunteering for communities:

- **Build Climate Resilience** – Help communities adapt to environmental challenges like bushfires.
- **Improve Public Spaces** – Contribute to healthier national parks and green spaces.
- **Strengthen Community Ties** – Foster collaboration between businesses and local conservation efforts.
