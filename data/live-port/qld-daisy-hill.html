Corporate Volunteering at Daisy Hill Conservation Park

Location: Daisy Hill Rd (approx. 25km from Brisbane CBD) Timing: Weekdays, typically 8:30am – 2:00pm

Bring your team together for a hands-on corporate volunteering opportunity in Brisbane at Daisy Hill Conservation Park, one of Southeast Queensland's most important conservation areas.

Surrounded by eucalypt forest and rich wildlife habitat, Daisy Hill plays a critical role in koala conservation and bushland protection. Corporate volunteering here gives your team the opportunity to contribute directly to wildlife habitat health while building connection, purpose and environmental awareness.

Why corporate volunteering at Daisy Hill matters?

Daisy Hill Conservation Park is a vital refuge in Southeast Queensland, supporting a large population of native wildlife and providing essential habitat for koalas.

In a boost for conservation, the Queensland Government recently announced a 213-hectare expansion of the park, significantly increasing protected land and opportunities for ecological restoration. This makes now an ideal time for organisations to get involved and support long-term conservation outcomes through volunteering.

The park also serves as an important space for environmental education and community connection. By volunteering at Daisy Hill, your team helps protect biodiversity while contributing to a healthier, more resilient landscape in QLD.

What your team will do

Corporate volunteering days at Daisy Hill are practical, educational and rewarding. Activities may include:

  • Learning to identify native plants and invasive weeds
  • Weeding and land care to support habitat health
  • Assisting with bushland restoration activities
  • Restoring bushland and wetland habitats
  • An exclusive visit to the Daisy Hill Koala Centre, learning about koalas, their habitats and current conservation challenges
