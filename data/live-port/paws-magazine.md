# PAWS Magazine
## Discover more about conserving Australia's national parks

In 2020, we decided to align with our values and to be as environmentally conscious as possible by eliminating the use of paper and printing in favour of digital delivery of our newsletters.

We appreciate your ongoing support and hope that our e-newsletter will continue to be a valuable source of information and inspiration for you.
