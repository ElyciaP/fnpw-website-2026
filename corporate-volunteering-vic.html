Get involved

Ready to get into the field?

Let’s make it happen. Tell us where you’re based, how many people are on your team and when you’d like to get out there, or call our team direct for a chat.

1800 898 626
How it works
  1. Tell us what you need Share your state, team size and preferred dates.
  2. Choose your experience We’ll send you options that suit your team.
  3. Get ready to get outdoors Once you’ve chosen your experience, we’ll sort the details. You just need to show up in closed shoes and a hat, and get stuck in.
