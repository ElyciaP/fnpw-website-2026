1,000,000Trees planted, and counting
Landscape Resilience Program

One million trees in the ground, and nine million on the way.

What began after the 2019–20 bushfires as a promise to plant one million trees has become one of Australia’s largest community-led nursery restoration programs. Local nurseries grow seedlings from indigenous species, and volunteers plant them in national parks and on public and private land.

1.2M+ plantedGoal: 9M by 2030
Planting the millionth tree at Lane Cove National Park, October 2024.

In October 2024 we planted the millionth tree at Lane Cove National Park. Now we are working toward 9 million trees by 2030, restoring habitat, reconnecting wildlife corridors and building landscapes that can withstand fire, flood and drought.

In good company

Partnership in focus

Six years of helping the bush recover.

After the 2019–20 bushfires, L’OCCITANE Australia wanted to help put the landscape back. In July 2020, through the L’OCCITANE Ecosystem Restoration Fund, they joined our mission to plant one million trees and helped fund our first Bushfire Recovery Nurseries.

Their team has potted seedlings alongside nursery volunteers, their support helped the program reach its millionth tree, and six years on the partnership is restoring a Ramsar-listed wetland on Hindmarsh Island (Kumerangk) in South Australia.

Government partners

Protecting land for good, with every level of government.

We work with local, state and federal governments to gain permanent protection for habitats and species. You can see the outcomes on our projects page.

See our government partnerships

Australian Government

Through the Australian Government Caring for Country program, we managed the acquisition of over 530 properties for the Heritage Estates project, incorporating them into the national reserve system and increasing the size of Jervis Bay National Park. In 2020, following the tragedy of the Black Summer fires, we were part of the Australian Government Bushfire Response through the Wildlife Heroes project, supporting wildlife volunteers across Australia.

NSW Department of Planning, Industry and Environment

From our inception we have worked to support the NSW government to protect and conserve the state’s natural heritage. To date the partnership has secured over 50 land acquisitions, significantly increasing the area of the state reserve system. Throughout our history we have supported community, conservation and cultural heritage projects in partnership with the National Parks & Wildlife Service in NSW.

SA Department of Environment and Water

For over 20 years we have worked to support national parks and conservation projects in South Australia. Through partnership with the SA Department of Environment and Water we continue to support land acquisitions for national parks and large scale conservation projects, including the Remarkable Southern Flinders project that commenced in 2020.

QLD Department of Environment and Science

Working closely with the Queensland Parks and Wildlife Service, we aim to support long term conservation works involving Traditional Owner groups, and to identify areas of high biodiversity for potential land acquisitions for the state’s reserve system.

WA Department of Biodiversity, Conservation and Attractions

In 2020 we partnered with the Western Australian Government to support the Wildlife Heroes Rehabilitation and Emergency Grants. Through the partnership, over 60 volunteer wildlife rescue and rehabilitation grants were distributed across the state.

Partnership FAQs

Questions we often hear.

Can’t see your question? Ian and the partnerships team are happy to talk it through.

Ask us directly
Can we choose the project, place or species?

Yes. We co-design every partnership, so you can focus on a particular landscape, state, species or one of our three pillars: Growing National Parks, Saving Species and Healing the Land. We will suggest projects that match your goals and are ready to fund.

How much do we need to invest?

Partnerships are scoped around your goals and budget, from a single site through to a multi-year program across several states. Once we understand what you want to achieve, we will put options in front of you.

Can our staff get involved?

Yes. Many partners build volunteering days into their partnership, from planting and nursery work to bush regeneration. See corporate volunteering for what a day looks like.

How will we know what our partnership achieved?

Every project is monitored and reported. You will receive quarterly updates and an annual impact report, and we can align reporting to your own sustainability or ESG frameworks.

Can we talk about the partnership publicly?

We hope you will. We create co-branded stories and assets with you, and we agree approvals together so every message is accurate and on brand for both of us.

How do we get started?

Send us an enquiry using the form below or email Ian Laing, our Chief Commercial Officer, at ilaing@fnpw.org.au. We will set up an initial conversation to understand your goals.
