Landscape Resilience Program

1.2 million trees in the ground

On the way to nine million by 2030. What began after the 2019–20 bushfires as a promise to plant one million trees has become one of Australia’s largest community-led nursery restoration programs.

1.2M+ planted · Goal: 9M by 2030

Thank you to every partner, nursery and volunteer who helped plant them.
Planting the millionth tree at Lane Cove National Park, October 2024.

In October 2024 we planted the millionth tree at Lane Cove National Park. Our goal is 9 million trees in total by 2030, restoring habitat, reconnecting wildlife corridors and building landscapes that can withstand fire, flood and drought.

1.2M+native trees planted since 2020
30+community-led nurseries in our network
2,500+hectares of critical habitat restored
“Planting our millionth tree was more than a milestone, it was a catalyst for change in our community. We witnessed firsthand how reforesting our land can renew both nature and community spirit.”
Ian Darbyshire, Chief Executive Officer, Impact Report 2024

In good company

Partnership in focus

Six years of helping the bush recover.

After the 2019–20 bushfires, L’OCCITANE Australia wanted to help put the landscape back. In July 2020, through the L’OCCITANE Ecosystem Restoration Fund, they joined our mission to plant one million trees and helped fund our first Bushfire Recovery Nurseries.

Alongside One Tree Planted, L’OCCITANE is one of the key partners behind a program that reached its millionth tree in 2024 and has restored more than 2,500 hectares of critical habitat. Six years on, the partnership is restoring a Ramsar-listed wetland on Hindmarsh Island (Kumerangk) in South Australia.

Partnership FAQs

Questions we often hear.

Can’t see your question? Ian and the partnerships team are happy to talk it through.

Ask us directly
Can we choose the project, place or species?

Yes. We co-design every partnership, so you can focus on a particular landscape, state, species or one of our three pillars: Growing National Parks, Saving Species and Healing the Land. We will suggest projects that match your goals and are ready to fund.

How much do we need to invest?

Partnerships are scoped around your goals and budget, from a single site through to a multi-year program across several states. Once we understand what you want to achieve, we will put options in front of you.

Can our staff get involved?

Yes. Volunteering days can be built into your partnership, from planting and nursery work to bush regeneration. See corporate volunteering for what a day looks like.

How will we know what our partnership achieved?

Every project is monitored and reported. You will receive quarterly updates and an annual impact report.

Can we talk about the partnership publicly?

We hope you will. We create co-branded stories and assets with you, and we agree approvals together so every message is accurate and on brand for both of us.

How do we get started?

Send us an enquiry using the form below or email Ian Laing, our Chief Commercial Officer, at ilaing@fnpw.org.au. We will set up an initial conversation to understand your goals.
