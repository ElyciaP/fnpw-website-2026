Partnership story

Nine million trees by 2028.

We have 1.2 million native plants in the ground so far. Our strategy takes that to nine million by 2028, restoring habitat, reconnecting wildlife corridors and building landscapes that hold up under fire, flood and drought.

Partners fund plantings at the scale that suits them, from a single site through to a multi-year program across several states. Every planting is monitored and reported, so you can see exactly what your investment put back.
