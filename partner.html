How we work

From first conversation to results you can report.

Whatever you choose to fund, the way we work stays the same, and your business has a clear role at every step.

  1. 01

    Identify

    With Traditional Custodians, scientists and government, we find the places and species where your support will make the biggest difference, then agree the scope, budget and milestones with you.

  2. 02

    Deliver

    We bring the partners and funding together and engage the specialists who do the work, from Traditional Owner rangers to community nurseries, against clear milestones. You can see the work on the ground for yourself.

  3. 03

    Demonstrate

    We track the ecological, financial and partnership outcomes and report back, so your business can stand behind the results in its own reporting.

Flagship program

Landscape Resilience: on the way to nine million trees.

What began after the 2019–20 bushfires as a promise to plant one million trees is now one of Australia’s largest community-led nursery restoration programs. We reached the millionth tree in 2024 and have set our sights on nine million by 2030. Partners fund the seed collection, nurseries and planting that restore habitat at the scale of whole regions.

Partnership in focus

Restoring a Ramsar wetland on Hindmarsh Island.

15 ha
restored on Hindmarsh Island in 2025
10,000
local provenance plants across five sites
2,500+ ha
of habitat restored across the wider program

On Hindmarsh Island (Kumarangk), inside the Coorong and Lower Lakes Ramsar Wetland, L’OCCITANE’s $90,000 contribution in 2025 helped restore coastal woodland and wetland alongside the Ngarrindjeri, the Kumarangk Hindmarsh Island Landcare Group, local nurseries and park managers.

The partnership began in July 2020, when L’OCCITANE Australia helped fund our first Bushfire Recovery Nurseries through the L’OCCITANE Ecosystem Restoration Fund. Alongside One Tree Planted, they are one of the key partners behind the program that reached its millionth tree in 2024.

“The key for us was to identify an environmental program partner that was genuinely aligned to our corporate value of respecting biodiversity. FNPW has a long track record of delivering meaningful sustainability outcomes, and we trust them to achieve measurable results on a national scale.”

Pierre Joffre, L’OCCITANE Australia

Also in 2025

Safer access through koala habitat at Bongil Bongil.

Gowing Bros Ltd · Bongil Bongil National Park, NSW

With NSW National Parks & Wildlife Service, we upgraded an existing boundary track using funding from Gowing Bros, owner of the adjoining land. The work improved safe public access while protecting habitat inside a declared Koala Asset of Intergenerational Significance.

Share of sales

Zorali

Now in its third year, our partnership with Zorali gives 10 per cent of the proceeds from each limited-edition fleece to our work. The July 2025 Sundown edition followed earlier releases, and the collaboration has raised more than $12,000 so far.

Matched checkout giving

Dicker Data

Dicker Data resellers can add a $1 seedling or a $10 tree to their online order at checkout, and Dicker Data matched reseller donations when the partnership launched, so every order helps fund native planting.

A contribution on every product

AnchorMark

For every bucket of its S2 screws sold (1,000 screws to a bucket), AnchorMark contributes $1 to our tree planting partnership, so a building product helps fund restoration.

Accountable at every step
