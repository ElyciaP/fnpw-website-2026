Since 1970

Fifty-five years of keeping Australia wild.

Our first acquisition was Mount King Station in north-west NSW, announced a month after our Foundation was established in 1970. Since then the work has grown from single land purchases into partnerships that protect and restore whole landscapes, and the land we have helped secure will stay protected for good.

Across our three pillars

Where the results came from.

Growing National Parks

Land secured for good.

We work with First Nations groups, governments and local partners to identify high-value land and bring it into the protected estate, where it stays safe for the long term.

Mundoo Island Station

Murray Mouth, SA

1,900 hectares added to Coorong National Park, home to 65 nationally listed threatened species.

Read the story

Undilla Station

Queensland

23,000 hectares secured, providing habitat for at least five threatened species.

Healing the Land

Habitat grown from the ground up.

Through our Landscape Resilience Program, community nurseries grow local native plants and community groups put them back into fire, flood and clearing-affected country.

Native Plant Nurseries

Four states

More than 30 community nurseries and 49 community groups have grown and planted 1,215,443 trees, shrubs and seedlings.

Read the story

Fire Wise

VIC, SA, NSW

14 community nurseries supported, more than 110,000 native plants grown, 145 living seedbanks and over 4,900 people trained.

Read the story
Saving Species

Recovery you can measure.

We back targeted recovery programs for the species that need it most, and we fund the volunteers and local groups who carry that work out in the field.

Curb Wombat Mange Program

New South Wales

18,582 treatments delivered, almost double the 10,000 target, reaching an estimated 1,498 wombats.

Read the story

Growling Grass Frog

Victoria

Between 35 and 40 frogs translocated to build a founding population of 60 to 65.
