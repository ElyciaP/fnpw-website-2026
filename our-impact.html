Mundoo Island, a once-in-a-generation win

On Ngarrindjeri Country we secured Ramsar-listed wetlands that support 65 nationally listed threatened species. A gift in a Will made it possible.

With the Ngarrindjeri Aboriginal Corporation and the Australian and South Australian governments

Saving Species
Case study · New South Wales
~1,498
wombats treated for mange

Curb Wombat Mange Program

$2 million in Rapid Response Grants funded the first large-scale targeted mange program of its kind in NSW, with 84% more wombats treated than before it began.

With Aboriginal organisations, volunteer groups, community carers and NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service

Healing Our Land

9 million trees for our Landscape Resilience Program

After the Black Summer fires we promised a million trees in five years. We passed it in 2024 with 30 community nurseries. Now we're going further, with 9 million trees in the ground by 2030 to restore habitat and make our landscapes more resilient.
