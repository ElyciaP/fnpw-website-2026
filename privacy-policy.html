Privacy policy.

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Effective November 2026

1. Purpose and scope

Foundation for National Parks & Wildlife (FNPW), ABN 90 107 744 771, respects the privacy of people who support, work with or engage with us. This policy explains how we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information, and how individuals may access or correct it or make a complaint. It applies to our websites, fundraising, conservation programs, partnerships, events and other activities. We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), where applicable.

2. What information we collect

Depending on your relationship with FNPW, we may collect your name, contact details, organisation, role, communication preferences, donation and engagement history, payment transaction details (generally handled by payment providers), volunteer or event participation, correspondence, and information relevant to grant applications, projects and partnerships. For conservation projects we may collect site addresses, property or location details, project contact details, monitoring records and other information needed to deliver and demonstrate outcomes. Website information may include device identifiers, IP addresses and analytics data. We collect sensitive information only where reasonably necessary and permitted by law, including obtaining consent where required.

3. How we collect information

We generally collect information directly from you through forms, donations, enquiries, events, meetings, correspondence and our websites. We may also receive information from authorised representatives, delivery partners, community nurseries, government agencies, professional advisers, public registers and publicly available sources where lawful and appropriate. Where reasonable, we explain why information is collected and any likely disclosures at or before collection. You may interact anonymously or using a pseudonym where lawful and practicable; however, some activities require identification.

4. Why we use information

We use personal information to process donations and issue receipts; administer fundraising, bequests and supporter relationships; communicate about our work and opportunities to support it; manage partnerships, grants, volunteering and events; assess and deliver conservation projects; map and monitor restoration outcomes; meet reporting, governance and legal obligations; improve our services and websites; manage enquiries and complaints; and protect people, systems and organisational assets. We use information for other purposes only where permitted by law, including with consent where required.

5. Conservation sites and location information

FNPW may require accurate restoration site locations and appropriate project contact information for project planning, mapping, monitoring, verification, acquittal, impact reporting and future stewardship. A site address may be personal information when it identifies or can reasonably identify an individual. We restrict access according to operational need and consider whether published maps or reports should use aggregated, approximate or de-identified locations. Delivery partners providing third-party information must have an appropriate basis to collect and share it and should give individuals suitable notice. This policy does not replace collection notices or data-sharing terms in partner agreements.

6. Who we share information with

Where reasonably necessary and permitted, we may disclose relevant information to contracted technology, payment, fundraising and communications providers; conservation delivery partners, nurseries and contractors; government agencies and funders for authorised project reporting; auditors, insurers and professional advisers; and regulators or other parties where required or authorised by law. We seek to limit disclosures to what is needed for the relevant purpose and apply suitable contractual and organisational safeguards. We do not sell personal information. We do not provide donor information to unrelated organisations for their own direct marketing without an appropriate legal basis.

7. Storage, security and retention

Information may be held in secure electronic systems, cloud platforms and, where necessary, physical records. FNPW takes reasonable steps to protect personal information against misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure, including access restrictions, staff awareness and appropriate service-provider controls. No electronic system is completely risk-free. We retain information only for as long as needed for lawful business, conservation, accountability and recordkeeping purposes, then securely delete or de-identify it where reasonable and legally permitted.

8. Overseas handling

FNPW uses technology platforms and service providers to support its operations, fundraising, communications and conservation programs. Some of these providers may store or process personal information outside Australia or provide access to information from overseas. Where FNPW discloses personal information to an overseas recipient, we take reasonable steps to protect that information and comply with our obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), including the Australian Privacy Principles. Where practicable, FNPW identifies the countries in which overseas recipients are likely to be located. These countries are the United States and Canada. FNPW maintains appropriate contractual, organisational and technical safeguards for personal information handled by its service providers.

9. Fundraising and communications

We may contact supporters and prospective supporters about conservation outcomes, appeals, events, partnerships and opportunities to give, where permitted by law. We may use engagement and donation information to make communications more relevant. You may opt out of promotional communications using the unsubscribe facility or by contacting us. Transactional, legal or essential project communications may still be sent where appropriate. We comply with applicable direct marketing and electronic messaging requirements.

10. Website, cookies and third-party links

Our websites may use cookies and similar technologies to operate website functions, understand traffic, measure campaign performance and, where configured, support relevant advertising. Some tools may collect online identifiers or enable third parties to receive information. The technologies actually in use, their purposes and available choices should be accurately described in a website cookie notice following a technical audit. You may adjust browser settings and other available consent controls, although some features may not function as intended. External websites and payment pages have their own privacy practices.

11. Artificial intelligence and automated tools

FNPW may use approved digital and AI-enabled tools to support administration, analysis, communications and project work. We apply human oversight and privacy safeguards and do not intentionally enter sensitive or confidential personal information into unapproved public AI services. If FNPW uses automated decision-making arrangements that trigger specific statutory disclosure requirements, we will update this policy as required by the applicable commencement dates.

12. Access and correction

You may request access to personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading. We will respond within a reasonable period and explain any lawful refusal. We may need to verify your identity. We do not charge for making a request; any permitted charge for providing access will be reasonable and explained in advance.

13. Privacy incidents and complaints

If we become aware of a privacy or security incident, we will assess and respond to it, including notifying affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) when required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. If you have a privacy concern, please contact our Privacy Officer. We will acknowledge and investigate complaints and aim to respond within 30 days. If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may be able to complain to the OAIC at www.oaic.gov.au.

14. Contact and updates

Privacy Officer, Foundation for National Parks & Wildlife, GPO Box 2666, Sydney NSW 2001. Telephone: 1800 898 626. Email: fnpw@fnpw.org.au

We may update this policy to reflect changes in law, systems and operations. The current version will be available on fnpw.org.au with its effective date.
