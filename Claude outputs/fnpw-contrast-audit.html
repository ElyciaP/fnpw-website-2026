A gap in the palette itself

No Wattle or Earth/tan tone is dark enough to carry small text on a light background.

Wattle’s darkest tone (#9F8506) reaches 3.60:1 on white. Earth/tan’s darkest (#AB7341) reaches 3.99:1. Both fall short of the 4.5:1 that body text needs, so any small text in those colours on cream, paper or white fails no matter which tone you pick. That single gap is behind issues 5, 6, 7 and 8.

This matters beyond the website, because Healing the Land is the wattle pillar and Wattle is a named primary. Three options: add a darker tone to each family in the guidelines, restrict those two families to headings and backgrounds only, or pair them with #241914 text as I have suggested above. The first is the real fix and worth raising when the guidelines are next revised.

What was excluded

39 flags were checked by eye and set aside: text on the purple campaign gradient and the dark hero photographs, where the audit could not resolve the painted backdrop, and the large outlined numerals, where a stroke rather than a fill supplies the contrast. Donate and Send enquiry buttons were left out by your direction, and the one CTA listed above is there for completeness only.

Thresholds are WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for text at 24px or above, or 18.7px and bold. Ratios were computed from rendered pixel colours, including transparency compositing, not from stylesheet values.
