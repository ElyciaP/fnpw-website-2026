Healing the Land

Native Plant Nurseries

NSW · VIC · QLD · SA · ACT   ·   Since 2020

Only a tenth of Australia’s native plants are available as seed. So we built the nursery network to grow the rest.

1mTrees plantedAcross four states, with the millionth going in during October 2024.
30+Community nurseriesGrowing local stock, with 58 community groups involved.
2,500Hectares restoredNative habitat brought back since the program began.

Figures from our Impact Report 2024.

Only about a tenth of Australia’s native plant species are commercially available as seed. After the Black Summer fires burnt 24 million hectares, that shortage stopped being an academic problem. You cannot restore what you cannot grow, and nobody was going to supply the rest at the scale the country suddenly needed.

Building the supply chain instead of buying from it

We started the Landscape Resilience Program to fix the bottleneck rather than work around it. Volunteers collect seed from local ecosystems. More than thirty community-led nurseries propagate it and bank what is rare or threatened. The plants then go back into degraded country as locally adapted stock rather than whatever happened to be in a catalogue.

The nurseries work with Landcare groups, First Nations partners and landholders, because the knowledge of what belongs where is held locally and cannot be centralised.

Where the plants go

The work concentrates on biodiversity hotspots, including two UNESCO World Heritage areas: the Gondwana Rainforests of Australia and the Greater Blue Mountains. Native plantings hold soil against erosion, feed and shelter wildlife, and leave the land better able to take the next fire, flood or drought.

Where it has got to

In October 2024 we planted the millionth tree, five years after the program started in the wake of the 2019 and 2020 fires. Two and a half thousand hectares have been restored, 296 plant species are being grown, and more than 25 threatened species have habitat because of it. One Tree Planted and L’Occitane Australia have been central to funding that scale.

The target now is eight million trees and a hundred community nurseries by 2030.
